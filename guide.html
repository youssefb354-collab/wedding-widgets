1Open your template

  1. Open the template link from your download PDF. Log in to Canva — a free account is enough.
  2. Click Use template. Canva makes your own private copy. Your edits never change the original.
  3. You'll see 13 pages: Welcome, Our Day, Save the Date, Our Story, The Day, The Venue, Travel & Stay, Dress Code, Moments, Gifts, Questions, RSVP, Thank You.
💡 Each page becomes one item in your website's menu. To rename a menu item, rename the page. Don't need a section? Delete the page.

2Make it yours

TextDouble-click any text to type. Names, dates, schedule, FAQ — everything is real, editable text.
PhotosDrag your photo from Uploads onto any photo — it drops into the same frame and crop.
ColoursClick an element → colour square in the toolbar. Tip: Change all recolours the whole site in one click.
FloralsThe watercolour flowers are separate images — move, resize or delete them freely.
📱 Keep things in one column when you add new content (text under text, not side by side). Canva turns columns into a phone layout automatically, and simple stacks always look best on phones.

3RSVP → your Google Sheet (optional, 5 min)

Guests reply once for their whole household — names, meal choice, allergies, shuttle and a song request. Every reply lands in your own Google Sheet, with a live summary tab (attending, meals, dietary notes).

  1. Go to sheets.new and name the sheet “Wedding RSVPs”.
  2. Menu Extensions → Apps Script. Delete what's there, then paste the script:
    Download .gs
  3. At the top of the script, set NOTIFY_EMAIL (to get an email for each reply) and COUPLE_NAMES. Click 💾 Save.
  4. Choose setup in the function menu → Run → Allow the permissions (it's your own script, in your own account).
  5. Deploy → New deployment → type Web app · Execute as Me · Who has access Anyone → Deploy. Copy the Web app URL.
  6. Paste it in the Link Builder under “Google Sheet RSVP link”.
✅ Test it: open your RSVP link, send a reply, and check that a new row appears in the sheet. Skipped this step? The RSVP form still works for testing, but replies stay on that phone only.

4Connect your buttons

Open the Link Builder, pick your design, and fill in names, date, venue and meals. It creates your personal links. Paste each one onto the matching button in Canva:

How to link in Canva: click the button → 🔗 Link icon in the toolbar (or right-click → Link) → paste → Enter.

Personal invitation links (optional)

At the bottom of the builder, list your households (e.g. The Smith Family; 4; John | Jane | Lily | Tom). Each gets a personal RSVP link that greets them by name, pre-fills the guests and limits the number of seats. You can also download QR codes for printed invitations and a CSV list.

Gifts, live stream, hotels

Link “Honeymoon fund”, “Watch live” and each “Book” button to your registry, stream or hotel page the same way.

5Preview & publish

  1. Click Preview (top right) → phone icon 📱 to see the mobile version. Tick Include a navigation menu if it isn't already.
  2. Click Publish website → choose a free address like amelia-and-james.my.canva.site → Publish.
  3. Optional privacy: Publish website → Settings → Password protection, then share the password on your invitations.
  4. Changed something later? Click Publish website again. Your address stays the same.
💌 Share your website link and QR code on invitations, save-the-dates or WhatsApp. For a custom domain (amelia-and-james.com) see Canva's domain settings.

Questions

Do I need Canva Pro?

No. Editing, the phone version, password protection and publishing on a free canva.site address all work on Canva Free.

Can music start automatically when guests open the site?

Canva websites don't autoplay audio, and phones block autoplay anyway. Use a “Play our song” button linked to your Our song link — one tap and it plays.

The countdown numbers in Canva don't move.

Canva pages are static, so those boxes are a styled picture of the countdown. Link them to your Live countdown link (step 4) and guests get the real ticking version.

Where are RSVPs stored? Is it private?

In your own Google Sheet, in your own Google account. Nobody else (including us) can see them. If you skip the Google Sheet step, test replies stay only on the phone that sent them.

Can guests change their answer?

Yes. If they reply again with the same email, the sheet replaces their earlier answer.

Other languages?

The RSVP form, calendar, countdown and music pages speak English, French, Spanish, German and Italian. Choose the language in the builder. Translate the Canva text by typing over it.

Something looks off on phones.

Usually two elements overlap. Make sure text and images don't sit on top of each other (except backgrounds), and keep new content in one column. Then check the phone preview again.
