How to connect a link in Canva

  1. Open your wedding website in Canva and click the button you want to connect (Reply now, Add to calendar, the countdown boxes, or any button you add for music).
  2. Click the link icon 🔗 in the top toolbar (or right-click → Link), paste the copied link, press Enter.
  3. Repeat for every button with the same job (for example, both Add to calendar buttons).
  4. Publish your website. Guests tap the button and the RSVP form, calendar, live countdown or music opens in your colours.
